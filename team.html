The name is false advertising. Ja'Marr Chase at 1, Nico Collins and Kyren Williams back to back, and — almost rudely — both Joe Burrow and Patrick Mahomes on the same roster.

Becca's Big Caulk

Bijan Robinson and Brock Bowers through two rounds is a genuinely strong foundation. Naturally, the follow-up was drafting both Dak Prescott and Kyler Murray at quarterback, just in case.

Between The Uprights
Manning

Jonathan Taylor and Kenneth Walker to open, then a full pivot into wide receivers for six straight rounds. The real story is Round 13: a kicker, drafted while the league was still filling starting lineups. Two rounds later, a punter. Peak vibes-over-value drafting, courtesy of the guy who runs this newsletter.

Maye 4 Microfiber
Joe Shapiro
Rebranded from Microfiber Shorts Acceptable

Christian McCaffrey at 1 is a bet on a man's ankles holding up for sixteen weeks, and the next several picks — Jeremiyah Love, Aaron Jones, Brian Robinson, Tyler Allgeier — read like insurance against that exact bet failing. Drake Maye at quarterback earned the team its new name; the running back room earned it a second look at the waiver wire.

Michael Magargee

Drafted Ashton Jeanty at pick 14, so at least the team name checks out. Followed it up by drafting two rookie quarterbacks, Jaxson Dart and Bo Nix, which is either a bold youth movement or the same coin flip called twice.

Chuba These Nuts
Dylan Melby

Puka Nacua and Chase Brown to start is a quietly efficient draft, right up until Caleb Williams and Jordan Love showed up as quarterback two and three. Nobody asked for a room this deep at quarterback, but here we are.

Victorious Secret
Victoria Cross

Omarion Hampton and Breece Hall gives this team real juice at running back, and George Kittle is a smart, if aging, luxury tight end pick. But the headline is Round 13: a punter, drafted three full rounds before anyone else in the league even considered one. Also: Daniel Jones and Bryce Young are the entire quarterback room. Choose your victories carefully.
